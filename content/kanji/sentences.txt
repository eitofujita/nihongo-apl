# Extra example sentences for kanji writing practice, for kanji the curriculum has few sentences for.
# Used by `npm run kanji` together with the lesson examples.
# kanji | sentence with furigana as 漢字{かんじ} | English | Russian

四 | りんごを四{よっ}つください。 | Four apples, please. | Дайте, пожалуйста, четыре яблока.
